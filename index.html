Engineering Formulas & Reference Criteria Guide
1. Screen Diagonal & Cabinet Count Formula:
  • Theoretical Diagonal (mm) = Diagonal in Inches × 25.4
  • For 16:9 Display: Width = Diagonal × (16 / √337), Height = Diagonal × (9 / √337)
  • Raw Cabinet Count = Width / CabinetWidth and Height / CabinetHeight
  • Cabinets must be integers; therefore, the nearest integer grid provides the closest buildable physical size.
2. Pixel Density Formula: Density = 1,000,000 / (Pixel Pitch in mm)² (dots per square meter).
3. Visual Acuity (Retina) Distance: Based on the standard minimum angle of resolution (MAR) of 1 arcminute (1/60th degree) for normal 20/20 human vision: Distance = Pitch (mm) / (2 × tan(0.5 / 60)) ≈ 3.438 × Pitch in meters.
4. Brightness & Optical Luminance:
  • 1 Nit = 1 cd/m²: (Candela per square meter) is the international standard unit of display luminance.
  • Indoor: 500–1000 nits provides comfortable illumination in controlled ambient light without eye strain.
  • Outdoor: 4,500–10,000 nits is necessary to cut through direct sunlight and prevent screen wash-out.
5. Heat Dissipation (BTU/hr): Calculated as BTU/hr = Watts × 3.412142. (1 Ton of Air Conditioning = 12,000 BTU/hr).
6. Current Draw (Amperes): Amps = Total Max Power (Watts) / Line Voltage (V).
